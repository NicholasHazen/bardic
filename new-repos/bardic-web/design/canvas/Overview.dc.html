Nouns
ListenerWho is using Bardic. Has their own places.
BookTitle, author, cover, chapters, unchangeable text. May sit in a series.
PlaceWhere a listener is in a book, and whether it is finished.
VoiceFree (on your Bardic computer) or premium (paid, cloud).
AudiobookA book voiced by one voice. A book can have several.
AudioThe sound of a chapter. Kept and reused, never overwritten.
PlanA priced proposal with a limit. The only way money is spent.
AllowanceA monthly spending ceiling shared by everyone.
DownloadA copy of an audiobook on one device, for offline play.
