Audio words
Ready

On this device
Downloaded to the device you are holding; plays offline.
Making
Being turned into audio now.
Not yet
Nothing made. Pressing play makes it as you go.
Downloading
Being copied to this device now.
Out of date
Downloaded, but the server has since made newer audio. Update to replace it.
Money words

PremiumA paid cloud voice. Always shows an estimate.
EstimateFrom published rates. Unknown stays “unknown”, never $0.
LimitThe most one plan can spend. Bardic stops and asks at the limit.
AllowanceThe most the whole Bardic can spend in a month.
