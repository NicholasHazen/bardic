Design system

Bardic kitchen sink

Every token and primitive, rendered by bardic/static/ui.js. Copy from this page, not from a sibling feature file.

Colour tokens

Semantic names in tokens.css. The legacy names (--paper, --green, --muted, --line) are aliases; new code uses these.

Status tones (text on its background)

Type, space, radius, shadow

Serif for story and reading surfaces (book text, chapter titles, character and voice names, section heads). Sans for operational surfaces (panels, runs, tables, settings).

Space (4px scale with 2px and 6px half steps)

Radius and control heights

Shadow

1 · Buttons

One primary action per surface. Sizes: small (32px) for dense rows, default (40px), large (48px) for the final action of a sheet or consent. Busy buttons are disabled and set aria-busy.

BardicUI.button({label:'Start listening', variant:'primary', size:'large'})
BardicUI.button({label:'Delete voice', variant:'danger', size:'small', attrs:{'data-id':id}})
BardicUI.button({label:'Run', variant:'primary', busy:true, busyLabel:'Running…'})

2 · Badges and status tones

One badge per status. The tone always comes from BardicUI.statusTone(state), the single map; never pick a colour per screen.

statusBadge(state) for common states

BardicUI.statusBadge('budget_limited')        // Budget limited, warn
BardicUI.badge('9 of 12 voiced', BardicUI.statusTone('partial'))

3 · Callouts and messages

A callout is a block that stays (what happened, what is kept, what to do). A message is a live line that changes; render it empty and fill it with setMessage.

BardicUI.callout({tone:'bad', title:'The run stopped', text:'Finished chapters are kept.', actions:BardicUI.button({label:'Retry failed'})})
node.innerHTML = BardicUI.message({id:'save-message'});
BardicUI.setMessage(document.getElementById('save-message'), 'Could not save. Your edits are still here.', {tone:'bad'});

4 · Section head

H2 names the job (not a tagline). The lead has at most two sentences. At most one Next.

BardicUI.sectionHead({eyebrow:'Book', title:'Analyze the story', lead:'Find the characters and who speaks each line. Nothing runs until you confirm an estimate.',
  actions:BardicUI.button({label:'Step settings', size:'small'}), next:{label:'Review 2 results', attrs:{'data-next':'review'}}})

5 · Steps

One ordered list, one state and one tone per step. The compact strip sits under the tabs; the card variant is the expanded view.

BardicUI.steps({label:'Book progress', steps:[{id:'analyze', label:'Analyze', state:'complete'}, …], next:{label:'Choose voices'}})
BardicUI.steps({variant:'cards', steps:[{id:'cast', label:'Cast', state:'partial', stateLabel:'9 of 12 voiced', detail:'…', uses:['Analyze'], current:true}]})

6 · Consent

Every paid action shows scope, what is sent where, and an estimate before it starts. Unknown cost says "Cost unknown", never $0. The confirm label carries the cost. A missing key or server disables the confirm and says why, with a Set up link.

panel.innerHTML = BardicUI.consent({scope:'Chapter 1, passages 4–32', sends:[{what:'Chapter text', where:'Gemini (Google)'}],
  estimate:{requests:3, cost:0.021}, confirm:'Record chapter'});
BardicUI.openConsent(panel.querySelector('[data-consent]'));   // scrolls into view and takes focus

7 · Choice

Exclusive choices are radio groups (one tab stop; arrow keys move and select). Toggles use aria-pressed. Looks: segmented, cards, chips. Try the keyboard here.

container.innerHTML = BardicUI.choice({kind:'cards', label:'Narration', name:'provider', value:'device',
  options:[{value:'device', label:'Device', hint:'Free · voices on the Bardic computer'}, …]});
BardicUI.bindChoices(container, change => save(change.name, change.value));

8 · Formatting

BardicUI.fmt: money, percent, plural. Missing numbers read "unknown".
